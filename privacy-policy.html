Privacy Policy for Al-trader

Al-trader takes visitor privacy seriously. This policy describes what information we collect and how we use it. It applies only to online activities on this website and not to offline data collection. Using our website constitutes consent to this Privacy Policy and agreement with its terms.

Information We Collect

Visitors are told when personal information is requested and why. Information may be gathered through:

  • Direct communication — name, email, phone number, message contents, attached files, and other details shared voluntarily
  • Account registration — contact details such as name, company name, address, email, and telephone number

How We Use Your Information

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the website — ensuring proper functioning
  • Improve, personalize, and expand the website — tailoring features and content to user preferences
  • Understand and analyze usage — analyzing interaction patterns and trends
  • Develop new products, services, features, and functionality
  • Communication — direct or partner-based customer service, updates, website information, and marketing
  • Email correspondence — updates, newsletters, and promotional content
  • Fraud detection and prevention — maintaining a secure environment

Log Files

Standard server log files record IP addresses, browser type, ISP, date/time stamps, referring/exit pages, and click counts. This data is not linked to personally identifiable information and is used for trend analysis, site administration, movement tracking, and demographic gathering.

Cookies and Web Beacons

Cookies store visitor preferences and pages visited, used to optimize the user experience by customizing content based on browser type and other information.

Advertising Partners Privacy Policies

Third-party ad servers or networks may use cookies, JavaScript, or web beacons in their ads and links on the site. These technologies measure ad campaign effectiveness and personalize advertising content. Al-trader has no access to or control over these cookies.

Third Party Privacy Policies

This policy does not apply to other advertisers or websites. Consult those third parties' own privacy policies for opt-out options. You can disable cookies through your browser settings.

CCPA Privacy Rights (Do Not Sell My Personal Information)

Under the CCPA, California consumers may request that a business disclose the categories and specific pieces of personal data collected, request deletion of their data, and request that their data not be sold. We respond to requests within one month.

GDPR Data Protection Rights

  • Access — request copies of personal data (a small fee may apply)
  • Rectification — correct inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Erasure — request deletion under certain conditions
  • Restrict processing — under certain conditions
  • Object to processing — under certain conditions
  • Data portability — request transfer of collected data to another organization or directly to you, under certain conditions

Requests are answered within one month.

Children's Information

Parents and guardians are encouraged to monitor children's online activity. Al-trader does not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13. Contact us immediately if a child has provided such information so it can be promptly removed.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated periodically. Changes are posted on this page and are effective immediately after posting.

Contact Us

If you have questions or suggestions about this policy, please visit our Contact Us page.
