To Avoid Risks:

1 Invest for the long term and don't try to get rich quickly. Build wealth slowly over many years.
2 Regularly add money, like monthly from each paycheck, to let your savings grow.
3 Don't panic when markets reverse. Instead, wait patiently and stop selling as the prices usually recover.
4 Spread your investments across many kinds of assets. Do not invest in the same asset.
5 Reinvest any earnings into your account to speed up growth and enjoy the trading.
6 Invest a fixed amount of money regularly using dollar cost averaging to help lower your risk.
7 As a new investor, don't borrow money or use leverage. Only invest what you can afford to lose.
8 Get help from professionals if you need it. Advisors can make plans to help you reach your goals.
